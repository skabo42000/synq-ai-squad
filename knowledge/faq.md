# Synq Logic: Frequently Asked Questions

## Do I need to be technical?
Not at all. We handle every part of the setup, and we explain things in plain English. If you can send an email, you can use what we build.

## Will automation replace my team?
No. It takes the repetitive, boring tasks off their plate so they can spend time on the work that needs a human touch, like serving clients and closing deals. And you don't need to hire more staff to handle busy periods: the repetitive work is taken care of automatically.

## How long does it take to get started?
Most businesses begin with one focused project that goes live in weeks, not months. From there you can add more whenever it makes sense.

## Will it work with the software I already use?
In most cases, yes. We connect the email, calendars, customer lists, booking, invoicing, and forms you already have, so nobody has to learn a whole new system.

## What does it cost?
It depends on what you want automated, so we build a plan around your needs and budget. A free strategy call is the fastest way to get a clear answer, and many owners find it pays for itself in the hours saved each week.

## What if a customer wants to talk to a real person?
That's always an option. Our assistants are designed to hand the conversation to you or your team smoothly whenever a human is the better choice.

## Is my customers' information safe?
Security is a top priority for us. We only connect the tools that are needed, and we walk you through how information is handled before anything goes live.
